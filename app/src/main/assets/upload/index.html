TV Home

Set up the TV's Home screen from here. Changes show on the TV straight away.

City for the weather

Search, then choose: the weather then follows that city ("set by hand").

OpenWeatherMap key

To get the weather from OpenWeatherMap, paste your API key (openweathermap.org › your account › API keys). It is saved on the TV only, is never shown in full, and goes into backups.

Wallpaper photos

Photos you add are ticked for Home straight away, cropped to the TV's shape (16:9). Untick any you don't want on Home; when several are ticked they take turns. A special day's own photo is chosen in the Special days tab.

Your own dates

Each day brings its own banner, lights and wallpaper. Only listed here, never on the TV, so a birthday can stay a surprise.

Test on the TV's Home

Choose a weather, a time of day, a banner and the lights, then tap Show on the TV: the TV opens Home exactly as it looks (over its Settings) and follows every change you make here. Home keeps the test for 15 minutes, with a "Test from the phone" label in the corner. Back on the remote returns to Settings.

Back up

Saves everything you set up (settings, apps and their order, special days, which photos are on Home) to a file on this phone. Keep it somewhere safe: it is what puts the TV back as it was after a reinstall.

Restore

Puts back everything from a backup file. What is on the TV now is replaced; photos are replaced only if the backup has them. The launcher restarts on the TV to finish.
